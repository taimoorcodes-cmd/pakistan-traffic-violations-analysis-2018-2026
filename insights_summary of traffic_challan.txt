
================================================
Pakistan Traffic Challan Analysis
Key Business Insights
Author: Taimoor Ghalib
Dataset: 827,700 rows | 2018-2026
================================================

1. NATIONAL FINANCIAL SUMMARY
------------------------------
Total Fines Imposed  = 1,515,639,970 PKR
Total Collected      = 833,030,731 PKR
Total Leakage        = 682,609,239 PKR
Recovery Rate        = 54.96%
Finding: Pakistan loses over 682 Million
PKR annually due to uncollected fines.
Nearly half of all imposed fines remain
permanently uncollected.

2. THE 55% SYSTEMIC CEILING
------------------------------
Every violation type shows 54-55%
compliance rate regardless of fine amount,
vehicle type, province, or enforcement
method.
Finding: Non-payment is not caused by
specific violations or locations. It is
a deeply rooted national infrastructure
failure affecting the entire collection
system uniformly.

3. SPEEDING: BIGGEST REVENUE BLEEDER
------------------------------
Total Challans  = 165,995
Leakage         = 160,385,923 PKR
Compliance Rate = 55.05%
Finding: Speeding is the single largest
source of uncollected revenue. Fixing
speeding fine collections alone would
recover over 160 Million PKR.

4. KARACHI DOMINATES LEAKAGE
------------------------------
Karachi Leakage = 170,033,980 PKR
Karachi + Speeding = 39,987,977 PKR
Karachi appears in 4 out of top 5
leakage hotspots nationwide.
Finding: Targeting Karachi specifically
for payment recovery would yield the
fastest multi-million PKR return.

5. MOTORCYCLES TOP OFFENDERS
------------------------------
Total Challans  = 289,448
Leakage         = 238,595,860 PKR
Paid Rate       = 55.08%
Finding: Despite being small vehicles,
motorcycles are the #1 source of traffic
violations and financial leakage.
Combined with cars they account for
443 Million PKR in lost collections.

6. 136% GROWTH IN VIOLATIONS
------------------------------
2018 Challans = 60,023
2025 Challans = 141,943
Growth        = 136% increase
Finding: Traffic violations have more
than doubled in 8 years. Despite this
massive enforcement growth the payment
compliance rate has never improved
beyond 55% in any single year.

7. PUNJAB LARGEST UNPAID PROVINCE
------------------------------
Punjab Unpaid Tickets = 117,211
Punjab Leakage        = 322,093,026 PKR
Finding: Punjab holds the highest
volume of unresolved tickets. A
targeted digital payment campaign
in Punjab alone could recover over
322 Million PKR.

8. CAMERAS CAPTURE MORE BUT COLLECT SAME
------------------------------
Camera Tickets  = 330,562 (40%)
Officer Tickets = 290,300 (35%)
Camera Compliance = 55.08%
Officer Compliance = 54.96%
Finding: Automated cameras generate
more tickets than physical officers
but achieve identical collection rates.
The bottleneck is the payment collection
system not the detection system.

9. Q2 HIGHEST SEASONAL LEAKAGE
------------------------------
Q2 Target Fines  = 414,467,080 PKR
Q2 Leakage       = 186,901,384 PKR
Finding: April to June is the peak
quarter for both enforcement activity
and financial leakage. Government
should focus maximum collection
efforts during this quarter.

10. WEEKDAY RUSH HOUR PROBLEM
------------------------------
Weekday Tickets = 130,000 per day
Weekend Tickets = 90,000 per day
Weekend speeding = only 27-28% of
weekday speeding volume.
Finding: Traffic violations are
primarily a weekday rush hour problem
not a weekend leisure problem.
Deployment of enforcement resources
on weekday mornings and evenings
would be most impactful.

================================================
KEY RECOMMENDATIONS
================================================
1. Launch digital payment reminder system
   targeting Karachi and Punjab first
   to recover 500M+ PKR fastest.

2. Introduce vehicle registration blocking
   for unpaid fines to force compliance
   beyond the 55% ceiling.

3. Scale camera enforcement in Q2
   (April-June) when violations peak.

4. Create motorcycle-specific fine
   collection campaigns as they are
   the single highest risk vehicle class.

5. Investigate why 8 years of enforcement
   growth has never improved the 55%
   compliance rate and audit the national
   payment processing infrastructure.
